Power Devices

ULVAC boasts the industry's top delivery record in the manufacture of IGBTs and other silicon power devices, and offers a wide range of manufacturing technology solutions for power devices. In addition, our cutting-edge manufacturing equipment utilizing wide bandgap semiconductors such as SiC (silicon carbide) and GaN (gallium nitride) contributes significantly to improving power conversion efficiency and energy efficiency.

The challenge

[SAMPLE COPY] Power electronics is shifting from silicon to wide-bandgap materials. SiC and GaN devices demand new deposition, implantation and etching processes: at production yield, not lab scale.
