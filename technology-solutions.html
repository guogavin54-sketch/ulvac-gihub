Technology Solutions

ULVAC combines vacuum technology with deposition, etching, implantation and thermal processes to serve production in 27 application fields: from semiconductors, displays and power devices to batteries, optical films and pharmaceuticals.

Semiconductor
Devices

Cutting-edge sputtering, dry cleaning, and dry etching technologies for logic and memory devices, with equipment supporting 300mm wafers.

Power Devices

ULVAC leads the industry in deliveries of equipment for IGBTs and other silicon power devices, offering a broad range of manufacturing solutions for power devices.

MEMS Devices

Supporting a wide range of MEMS applications, including microsensors, microfluidic modules, optical switches, and bio-MEMS.

Packaging

ULVAC provides deposition and etching technologies for next-generation packaging processes such as SoC and SiP.

Displays
(LCD / OLED)

World No. 1 in LCD manufacturing equipment deliveries, plus end-to-end OLED solutions from pre-treatment and deposition to encapsulation.

Secondary Battery

Manufacturing technologies for applications ranging from compact electronics and biomedical devices to large-scale battery components.

LED Devices

Delivering high brightness with low power consumption, LED devices play a key role in next-generation displays and lighting.

Optical Films

Tailored deposition solutions for multilayer optical coatings, including UV-cut, IR-cut, anti-reflection, and anti-smudge films.
